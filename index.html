Questions

Before you get in touch

What does Ivory Studios do?
We design and build websites, brand identities and custom business systems such as CRMs, client portals and booking tools, plus the SEO that helps people find you. Many clients combine two or more.
How much does a project cost?
Most website projects fall between $1,000 and $15,000 depending on scope. Brand and business-system projects are scoped individually. After a free discovery call we send a fixed-price proposal within 48 hours.
How long does a project take?
A typical marketing website takes three to six weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger web apps and business systems take longer. Your proposal includes a clear timeline.
Where are you based? Do you work with international clients?
We are based in Cairo, Egypt, and work with clients worldwide over video calls, email and a shared client portal. We work in English and Arabic.
What happens on the discovery call?
Thirty minutes to understand your business, your goals and what is getting in the way. You leave with honest advice whether or not we work together, and we follow up with a proposal if it makes sense.

Let’s build what’s next.

Tell us about your business and where you want it to go. In 30 minutes you’ll have honest advice and a clear next step.
