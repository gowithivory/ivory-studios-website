About Ivory Studios

Frequently asked

Who is Ivory Studios?
Ivory Studios is a premium web design and development agency based in Cairo, Egypt, working with ambitious businesses worldwide. We design and build fast, conversion-focused websites, brand identities, e-commerce stores, and SEO that grow your business.
What does Ivory Studios do?
Ivory Studios offers custom web design, web development, brand identity, e-commerce, and SEO. Every Ivory Studios website is custom-built — no templates — and engineered to load fast, rank well, and turn visitors into customers.
Where is Ivory Studios based?
Ivory Studios is based in Cairo, Egypt, and works with clients worldwide. We collaborate over email, calls, and a shared client portal, so your location is never a barrier to working with us.
Is Ivory Studios a web design or web development agency?
Both. Ivory Studios is a full-service web design and development studio — we handle strategy, design, and engineering under one roof, so your website looks world-class and performs like it.
How do I get a quote from Ivory Studios?
Book a free 30-minute discovery call and we'll send a clear plan and a fixed-price proposal within 48 hours — no pitch, no pressure. You can also email teams@ivorystudios.io anytime.
