Web Design FAQ

How much does a custom website design cost?
Most design projects run $1,000–$15,000 depending on scope — a focused landing page at the lower end, a full multi-page site at the higher end. You get a fixed-price proposal after a free discovery call.
Do you use templates or design from scratch?
Every Ivory Studios website is custom-designed for your brand and goals. No templates — your site is built to stand out, not blend in.
How long does a web design project take?
A typical design phase takes 2–3 weeks, with full design-to-launch in 3–6 weeks. You approve every page before we build.
