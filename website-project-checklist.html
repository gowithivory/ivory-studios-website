Free guide

Plan your website project.

Ten questions to answer before you hire anyone. Working through them makes every quote clearer, every project faster and the finished site better at its job.

0 of 30 ready

Tick items as you go. Your progress stays in this browser only.

01

What should the website achieve?

Most sites try to do everything and end up doing nothing well. Pick the one result that matters most.

02

Who is it for?

Your website is a conversation with your best customers. The clearer you are about them, the less the site has to say.

03

Why should they choose you?

People compare. Give them reasons they can check.

04

Which pages do you need?

Structure comes before design. If you have a site already, start from what it does well.

05

Who will write and photograph it?

Content arriving late is one of the most common reasons website projects slip.

06

Is your brand ready?

A website amplifies your brand. If the brand is unclear, the website will be too.

07

What does it need to do?

Features are where budgets grow. List them now so every quote covers the same scope.

08

Who looks after it after launch?

A site is never finished. Decide now who keeps it current.

09

What are your budget and timeline?

A range is enough. It lets a studio recommend the right scope instead of guessing.

10

Who makes the decisions?

Projects move at the speed of feedback.
