A demonstration SaaS interface: an AI-powered analytics product where insights arrive as sentences, not just charts — designed to show how we approach dense, data-heavy products.

The Challenge

AI dashboards drown users in widgets. The value is in the answer, but most products make you assemble it yourself from a dozen panels.

Our Solution

An insight feed that leads with plain-language findings, drill-down charts one click behind them, and a component system with dark-mode-first design tokens the product team can scale.
