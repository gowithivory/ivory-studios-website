Pulseboard AI

A concept analytics dashboard that explains itself in plain English.

ClientConcept (Ivory Studios)
SectorSaaS product
Year2026
ScopeUI/UX Design, SaaS, Design System

Concept study. A self-initiated project by Ivory Studios to show how we would approach this kind of business. It is not client work, and the figures below are design targets, not results.

A concept SaaS interface where insights arrive as sentences, not just charts, showing how we approach dense, data-heavy products.

The challenge

Analytics dashboards drown users in widgets. The value is the answer, but most products make you assemble it yourself from a dozen panels.

Our approach

An insight feed that leads with plain-language findings, drill-down charts one click behind them and a component system with dark-mode-first design tokens.
