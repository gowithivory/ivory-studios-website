BrightSmile Dental

A concept clinic website that books patients while the front desk sleeps.

ClientConcept (Ivory Studios)
SectorHealthcare
Year2026
ScopeWeb Design, Online Booking, Local SEO

Concept study. A self-initiated project by Ivory Studios to show how we would approach this kind of business. It is not client work, and the figures below are design targets, not results.

A concept study for modern clinics: calm, trustworthy design, treatments explained in plain language, and booking that works at 11pm, when people actually decide to book.

The challenge

Most clinic sites are brochures with a phone number. Every after-hours visitor is a lost patient, and clinical jargon does nothing for an anxious one.

Our approach

Treatment pages written for patients, transparent price ranges, a slot-based booking flow with SMS reminders and local-SEO structure for 'near me' searches.
