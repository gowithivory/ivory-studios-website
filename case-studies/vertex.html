A concept property portal built to turn browsers into qualified enquiries.

Year2024

Concept study. A self-initiated project by Ivory Studios to show how we would approach this kind of business. It is not client work, and the figures below are design targets, not results.

A concept study for agencies and developers: a working listings portal with real search and a pipeline that captures enquiries while they're warm.

The challenge

Listings often live in a third-party iframe with no search, no filters and no way to see which properties drive enquiries.

Our approach

A custom portal with map and filter search, saved-property accounts, lead capture on every listing and analytics that show the team what is converting.
