APadel

One home for Egypt’s competitive padel scene: rankings, registration and results.

ClientAPadel
SectorSports platform
Year2026
ScopeWeb App, UI/UX Design, Development, Branding

APadel came to us with a spreadsheet and a clear idea: turn Egypt’s scattered club tournaments into one ranked, trackable circuit. We designed and built the whole platform: the public site, player rankings, tournament registration and an organiser back-office.

3
Roles: player, organiser, admin
Auto
Ranking points after each event
1
Platform replacing chats and spreadsheets

The challenge

Padel was growing fast locally but had no central home. Players competed without unified rankings or match history, and organisers ran registration, draws and results by hand across chats and spreadsheets.

What we built

A single platform with live rankings, automatic point calculation, doubles registration with shareable team codes and a role-based admin panel. It was designed mobile-first, because players register and check results from their phones.

Key features

  • Live player rankings, with points calculated automatically after each event
  • Doubles tournament registration with shareable team codes
  • An organiser back-office for registrations and results
  • Separate access for players, organisers and admins
  • Mobile-first, because players register and check results on their phones
  • Row-level security, so each person sees only their own data
