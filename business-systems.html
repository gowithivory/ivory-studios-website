When the business runs on spreadsheets and chats

As a business grows, the work spreads across spreadsheets, inboxes and group chats. Leads slip, clients chase updates and nobody has the full picture. Off-the-shelf tools rarely fit, so teams bend their process around the software. We design and build systems that fit your process instead.

Who it’s for

  • Teams tracking leads and clients in spreadsheets
  • Service businesses that want clients to see progress without chasing
  • Operators running bookings, events or memberships by hand

What you receive

  • Process mapping and system design
  • Custom CRM and sales pipelines
  • Client portals and member areas
  • Booking, scheduling and operations dashboards
  • Integrations and automations with the tools you already use
  • Roles, permissions, data migration and training

Questions

Common questions

Why custom instead of an off-the-shelf CRM?
Off-the-shelf tools are a good fit for many teams, and we will say so if that is the case for you. Custom makes sense when your process is a competitive advantage, when you pay for features you never use, or when you need several tools to talk to each other.
Can it connect to the tools we already use?
Usually, yes. We integrate with email, calendars, payment providers, spreadsheets and most services that offer an API.
Who owns the system and the data?
You do. The code, the database and the data are yours, and we document everything so it is never locked to us.
