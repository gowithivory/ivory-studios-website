Questions

Common questions

How much does a project cost?
Most website projects fall between $1,000 and $15,000 depending on scope. A focused landing page sits at the lower end; a multi-page site, web app or online store at the higher end. Brand and business-system projects are scoped individually. After a free discovery call we send a fixed-price proposal.
Which service should I start with?
Start with whatever is holding the business back most. If people can't find or understand you, that's usually the website. If your team is drowning in admin, it's a business system. The discovery call is there to work this out together.
Do you work with clients outside Egypt?
Yes. We're based in Cairo and work with clients worldwide over video calls, email and a shared client portal, in English and Arabic.
Do you offer support after launch?
Yes: maintenance, improvements and ongoing growth work. You can raise requests and follow progress in your client portal.
