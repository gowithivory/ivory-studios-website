The website now tells search engines clearly that it is "Ivory Studios" (logo text, homepage heading, footer, titles, structured data). The steps below are the ones only you can do. Do them in this order; each one strengthens the next. Expect changes to show over days to weeks. No one can guarantee a #1 position for a name other businesses also use (for example ivorystudios.ca, a music school), but these steps give Google the strongest possible signal that the Ivory Studios in Cairo is you.

## 1. Google Business Profile (biggest impact for searches in Egypt)

**First, check you're eligible.** Google only allows a profile for businesses that make in-person contact with customers: either clients visit you at an address, or you meet them at theirs. If you never meet clients in person, don't create one; it can be suspended. If you do meet clients in Cairo, continue.

1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in with the Google account you'll keep long term (ideally the teams@ivorystudios.io Workspace account).
2. **Business name:** `Ivory Studios`. Only the real name. Adding words such as "Web Design Cairo" breaks Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.
3. **Category:** primary `Website designer`. Add `Graphic designer` and `Software company` as additional categories if they fit the work you take.
4. **Location:**
   - If clients come to an office: answer **Yes** to "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" and enter the address.
   - If you go to clients instead: answer **No**, then add your service areas (for example Cairo and Giza). Your address stays private but is still needed for verification.
5. **Contact:** website `https://ivorystudios.io`, and a phone number if you want calls.
6. **Verify.** Google will offer a method, usually a **video**: record with the Business Profile app showing your location or signage, and proof you run the business (such as a business registration or a bill in the business name). Sometimes it's a postcard to your address (up to about two weeks).
7. Once verified, complete the profile:
   - **Description** (ready to paste, under 750 characters):
     > Ivory Studios is a design and technology studio in Cairo, working with clients worldwide. We design and build websites, brand identities and custom business systems such as CRMs, client portals and booking tools, plus the SEO that helps the right people find you. One team takes you from strategy to launch at a fixed price agreed before work starts. Book a free 30-minute discovery call and we'll send a fixed-price proposal within 48 hours.
   - **Services:** Web design, Web development, Brand identity, CRM & business systems, SEO & growth.
   - **Hours:** your real working hours.
   - **Logo and cover:** your logo, plus real photos of you or your work. Avoid stock photos.
   - **Opening date:** 2022.
8. **Reviews:** ask real clients (such as APadel) for a Google review. Never write, buy or swap reviews; Google removes them and can penalise the profile.

## 2. Google Search Console (tell Google about the changes)

Your domain already has a Google verification record, so a Search Console property probably exists. Sign in at **search.google.com/search-console** with the account that set it up.

1. **Sitemaps:** submit `https://ivorystudios.io/sitemap.xml`.
2. **URL inspection:** inspect `https://ivorystudios.io/`, then click **Request indexing**. Do the same for:
   - `https://ivorystudios.io/website-cost-guide`
   - `https://ivorystudios.io/website-project-checklist`
3. **Performance:** after a week or two, filter queries containing "ivory studios" to see impressions and position. This is the honest measure of progress.
4. Optional: in **Bing Webmaster Tools**, use "Import from Google Search Console" to cover Bing too.
