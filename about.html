About

A small studio with a high standard.

Ivory Studios is a design and technology studio in Cairo. We help ambitious businesses bring their vision to life online, with websites, brands and systems built to grow with them.

Our story

Ivory started with a simple belief: good businesses deserve digital work as good as they are.

Ammar founded Ivory Studios in 2022 after watching capable founders lose ground to competitors with a sharper online presence. The idea was straightforward: design and engineering should serve the business, not just look good in a portfolio.

That still shapes every project. We start by understanding how a business makes money and what is getting in its way, then design and build what moves it forward, whether that is a new website, a clearer brand or a system that takes the admin off a team’s plate.

We keep the studio deliberately small, so the people you meet are the people doing the work.

What we do: web design, web development, brand identity, CRM & business systems and SEO & growth.

Principles

What we believe

Clarity before decoration

A beautiful site that confuses people is a failure. We make sure visitors understand you first, then make it memorable.

Speed is a feature

Fast pages keep visitors and help search rankings. Performance is designed in from the first line of code.

Plain, honest communication

No jargon and no disappearing acts. You always know what’s happening and can follow it in your portal.

Details people feel

Spacing, wording, loading, focus states: the small things customers never mention but always notice.

Process

From first call to launch

Every project follows the same transparent path, with a fixed scope and price agreed before work begins.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We learn how your business works, who you serve and what success looks like. You get a written plan with scope, timeline and a fixed price.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes first, then high-fidelity designs you review and approve. Nothing is built until you are happy with it.

    Weeks 2–3
  3. 03

    Build

    We develop, test on real devices and share progress in your client portal as we go.

    Weeks 3–6
  4. 04

    Launch & grow

    We launch, monitor and fix anything that surfaces, then stay on hand for improvements.

    Ongoing

Typical timeline for a marketing website. Brand and systems projects are scoped individually.
