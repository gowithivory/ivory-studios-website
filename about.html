Who We Are

A small studio
with a big standard.

Ivory Studios is a Cairo-based web design and development studio. We partner with ambitious businesses to build digital experiences that look world-class and perform even better.

Our Story

Ammar started Ivory because good businesses deserved better websites.

In 2022, after watching capable founders lose deals to competitors with slicker online presence, Ammar launched Ivory Studios around a single bet: design and engineering should serve a measurable result — not just make a portfolio look impressive.

Too many agencies hand their clients slow, templated sites and call it strategy. We don't. Every project starts with a conversion goal and ends with the code to hit it — clean, fast, and built to last longer than the next trend cycle.

Today we work with clients across Egypt and around the world — from sports platforms to boutiques to SaaS products — and the standard never changes.
