Ivory Studios is a Cairo-based web design and development studio. We partner with ambitious businesses to build digital experiences that look world-class and perform even better.

Our Story

We started Ivory because most websites were settling.

Too many businesses were handed slow, templated sites that looked fine and did nothing. We knew a website could be more than a brochure — it could be the hardest-working member of your team.

So we built a studio around one idea: design and engineering should serve a result. Every pixel, every line of code, every page exists to move someone closer to a decision. That's the difference between a website that exists and one that converts.

Today we work with clients across Egypt and around the world — from sports platforms to boutiques to SaaS products — and the standard never changes.
