03 · Brand Identity

An identity people recognise and remember.

Positioning, logo and a complete visual system, designed to make your business distinctive and consistent wherever customers meet it.

Discuss your project See our work

When the brand looks smaller than the business

If your logo, colours and tone change from one place to the next, customers notice, even if they can't say why. A clear identity system makes you look established, helps people remember you and saves your team from reinventing the look every time they make something.

Who it's for

  • New businesses that want to launch looking established
  • Growing companies whose brand has drifted across channels
  • Teams preparing a rebrand alongside a new website

What you receive

  • Brand discovery and positioning
  • Logo suite and usage rules
  • Colour, typography and imagery direction
  • Brand guidelines document
  • Social, presentation and document templates
  • Design tokens ready for your website
